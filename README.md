# 📊 Business Insights 360 | Power BI Dashboard

An end-to-end Business Intelligence project designed to analyze business performance across Finance, Sales, Marketing, and Supply Chain, with an Executive View for strategic decision-making.

The project uses interactive Power BI dashboards to transform business data into actionable insights, helping stakeholders monitor KPIs, evaluate profitability, analyze market performance, and identify operational challenges.

## 📌 Project Overview

AtliQ Hardware is a growing hardware business that needs a comprehensive view of its performance across different business functions.

**Business Insights 360** brings key business metrics together in an interactive dashboard, enabling users to analyze revenue, gross margin, net profit, forecast accuracy, inventory risks, customer performance, and product performance.

### 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze financial performance and profitability.
- Monitor sales trends and customer contributions.
- Evaluate product performance and market share.
- Track forecast accuracy, net errors, and inventory risks.
- Identify opportunities to improve revenue and operational efficiency.
- Support data-driven business decisions through interactive visualizations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** — Interactive dashboards and business reporting
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — Measures, calculated metrics, and KPI analysis
- **Data Modeling** — Relationships and analytical model design
- **Excel** — Data inspection and supporting analysis
- **Business Intelligence** — KPI monitoring and insight generation

## 📈 Dashboard Views

### 1. Finance View
Analyzes the company's financial health and profitability.

**Key features:**
- Net Sales, Gross Margin, and Net Profit KPIs
- Profit and Loss statement
- Revenue and profitability trends
- Year-over-year and benchmark comparisons
- Regional and product-segment performance

### 2. Sales View
Evaluates customer performance and sales effectiveness.

**Key features:**
- Customer revenue and gross margin analysis
- Customer performance matrix
- Net Sales and Gross Margin comparisons
- Unit economics analysis
- Identification of high- and low-performing customers

### 3. Marketing View
Evaluates product and market performance to identify growth opportunities.

**Key features:**
- Product performance analysis
- Gross Margin and Net Profit analysis
- Regional and market-level comparisons
- Product category performance
- Market share trend analysis

### 4. Supply Chain View
Monitors forecast performance and inventory-related risks.

**Key features:**
- Forecast Accuracy
- Net Error and Net Error Percentage
- Customer-level forecast performance
- Product-segment inventory risk
- Excess Inventory and Out-of-Stock (OOS) analysis

### 5. Executive View
Provides a consolidated overview of business performance for senior management.

**Key features:**
- High-level business KPIs
- Revenue and profitability trends
- Top-performing customers and products
- Revenue contribution by channel and division
- Regional performance comparisons
- Market share trends and business insights

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

The following findings are based on the project's analysis and supporting insight documents.

**1. Revenue Growth**

- The analysis reports substantial growth in gross sales and gross margin during 2020 and 2021 compared with the previous years.
- These trends help stakeholders evaluate business growth over time.

**2. Profitability Challenges**

- The analysis reports a decline in Net Profit Percentage from approximately -6.63% in 2021 to -13.98% in 2022.
- This highlights the importance of monitoring operating expenses and identifying opportunities to improve profitability.

**3. Market Share Analysis**

- The project reports an overall market share of approximately 5.9% in 2022.
- India recorded a market share of approximately 13.3%, making it a key market in the analysis.

**4. Supply Chain Risks**

- The analysis identifies inventory availability issues affecting certain PC product categories.
- Improving inventory planning and forecast accuracy can help reduce out-of-stock situations and protect sales opportunities.

**5. Customer and Channel Performance**

- Customer and channel-level dashboards help identify revenue concentration and opportunities to improve contributions from underperforming customers and sales channels.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following actions could help improve business performance:

- **Improve Profitability:** Review operating expenses and identify opportunities to reduce unnecessary costs.
- **Optimize Inventory:** Improve demand forecasting and inventory planning to reduce excess inventory and out-of-stock situations.
- **Strengthen Market Performance:** Investigate gross margin gaps and identify opportunities to improve performance in weaker regions.
- **Improve Sales Contributions:** Work with underperforming customers and sales channels to identify opportunities for revenue growth.
- **Monitor Business KPIs:** Regularly track revenue, gross margin, net profit, and forecast accuracy to support timely decisions.
